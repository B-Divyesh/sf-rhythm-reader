Privacy

Effective 27 August 2026. Rhythm Reader is local-first. It has no advertising, analytics, user account, or third-party tracking script.

What stays on your device

Your trainer settings, device timing offset, daily drill counts, scores, and optional purchase license are stored in your browser’s local storage. Tap timestamps and microphone audio are processed live in the browser. Audio is never recorded, retained, or uploaded.

Microphone

Microphone access is optional and begins only after you choose mic input and start a take. It is used for clap onset detection. The stream stops after the take. You can use screen or keyboard taps without granting access.

Purchases

If you buy or restore the style pack, checkout and license verification are handled by Sociobot/Dodo, the merchant of record. Their service receives the information needed to take payment and verify the license. Rhythm Reader sends only the license token during verification.

Your control

Clear this site’s browser data to erase its local practice data and stored license. Keep a separate copy of your purchase license before clearing it. Questions may be sent to privacy@sociobot.in.
